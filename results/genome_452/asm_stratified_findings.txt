ASM (per-CpG haplotype methylation) - stratified analysis
==========================================================
Method: modkit haplotype pileups (hp1/hp2) per sample (all 452, via copyq);
per-region Mann-Whitney U on per-CpG methylation rates + pooled Fisher exact.

Design: 3000 variants across 4 fine-mapping PIP strata (750 each),
MATCHED on mQTL association strength (median -log10p ~17-20 per stratum).

=== ASM by PIP stratum (mean fraction of 452 samples MWU-significant) ===
  PIP>0.9      : 0.058
  PIP 0.5-0.9  : 0.062
  PIP 0.1-0.5  : 0.051
  PIP<0.1      : 0.058
  Kruskal-Wallis H=30.06, p=1.3e-6 (small, NON-monotonic effect;
  PIP 0.5-0.9 highest, not PIP>0.9).

=== Cohort ASM consistency (stratified set, 2981 regions) ===
  Top region chr20:30845403: ASM (MWU) in 96% of samples.
  9 regions ASM in >50% samples; 66 in >25%; 525 in >10%.

FINDING: ASM prevalence is broadly comparable across fine-mapping PIP strata
(not increasing with PIP). Fine-mapping PIP and allele-specific methylation
capture partly DISTINCT signal: low-PIP variants (causal status unresolved by
LD) frequently still exhibit allele-specific methylation. ASM provides
orthogonal evidence complementing PIP-based prioritisation.

Validation: per-fragment read-level MWU on a matched stratified subset
(pipeline validated; single-molecule cross-check).
Full PIP>0.9 ASM (all 192,172 credible causal variants) in progress.
